# 💎 LUX NETWORKS
## 📜 OFFICIAL SERVER RULES
**Semi-Serious Roleplay | FiveM Community**

Welcome to **LUX Networks**, a semi-serious roleplay community where everyone is expected to create enjoyable, realistic and fair roleplay experiences.

While we encourage fun and creativity, all players must follow these rules to maintain a respectful and balanced community.

**⚠️ Failure to follow server rules may result in warnings, kicks, temporary bans or permanent bans.**

---

# 📌 1. GENERAL SERVER RULES

**1.1 — Respect All Players**
- Respect all community members, staff and emergency services.
- Harassment, bullying, racism, discrimination and hate speech are strictly prohibited.
- Toxic behaviour will not be tolerated.

**1.2 — No Cheating or Exploiting**
- Using cheats, mod menus, injectors, exploits or unauthorised third-party software is strictly prohibited.
- Duplication glitches and economy exploits must be reported immediately.
- Knowingly benefiting from exploits may result in a permanent ban.

**1.3 — No Metagaming (MG)**
- Using information obtained outside roleplay to benefit your character is prohibited.
- Information from Discord, streams or other external sources cannot be used in-game unless obtained through roleplay.

**1.4 — No Powergaming (PG)**
- Forcing unrealistic actions onto other players without allowing them to respond is prohibited.
- Players must roleplay injuries, situations and character limitations appropriately.

**1.5 — No Fail Roleplay (FailRP)**
- Players must maintain a reasonable level of realism.
- Unrealistic actions that negatively affect another player's roleplay are prohibited.
- Being a semi-serious server does not mean roleplay can be ignored.

**1.6 — No Random Deathmatch (RDM)**
- Attacking or killing another player without valid roleplay initiation or justification is prohibited.
- Verbal disagreements alone do not automatically justify violence.

**1.7 — No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)**
- Intentionally using a vehicle to attack or kill players without valid roleplay justification is prohibited.
- Vehicles cannot be used as weapons to avoid proper roleplay.

**1.8 — No Combat Logging**
- Disconnecting, force-quitting or deliberately crashing your game to avoid an active roleplay situation is prohibited.
- If you crash during active roleplay, reconnect as soon as reasonably possible.

**1.9 — No Stream Sniping**
- Watching another player's livestream to locate, track or gain information about their character is prohibited.

**1.10 — Staff Decisions**
- Staff instructions must be followed.
- Staff decisions can be appealed through the appropriate Discord ticket system.
- Staff abuse or impersonation is prohibited.

---

# 🎭 2. ROLEPLAY RULES

**2.1 — Value Your Life (NVL)**
- Your character must value their own life.
- If your character is placed in a genuinely life-threatening situation, you must react appropriately.
- Unrealistic disregard for your character's safety is prohibited.

**2.2 — Fear Roleplay (FearRP)**
- Players must realistically respond to threatening situations.
- Ignoring credible threats without valid roleplay reasoning is prohibited.

**2.3 — New Life Rule (NLR)**
- If your character respawns following an RP death, they cannot remember the events directly leading to that death.
- Players cannot immediately return to the same situation to seek revenge.
- Medical treatment and revival do not automatically trigger NLR.

**2.4 — Revenge Killing**
- Returning to attack or kill someone solely because they previously killed your character is prohibited.
- All future conflicts must have legitimate roleplay development.

**2.5 — Roleplay Initiation**
- Hostile roleplay must have clear and reasonable initiation.
- Players must have an opportunity to understand and respond to the situation.
- Initiation cannot be used as an excuse for random violence.

**2.6 — Third-Party Involvement**
- Players cannot randomly interfere in active criminal situations.
- Any involvement must have an established roleplay connection.

**2.7 — Character Development**
- Players are encouraged to develop believable characters and ongoing storylines.
- Your character's actions should have reasonable motivations and consequences.

**2.8 — Unrealistic Behaviour**
- Excessive trolling, constant disruption and deliberately ruining roleplay are prohibited.
- Players must not abuse game mechanics to perform unrealistic actions.

**2.9 — Roleplay Over Winning**
- Roleplay is about creating stories, not winning every situation.
- Losing a fight, arrest or pursuit is part of roleplay.
- Players must not deliberately manipulate rules to guarantee a win.

---

# 🚗 3. VEHICLE & DRIVING RULES

**3.1 — Realistic Driving**
- Driving should remain reasonably realistic for the situation.
- Reckless driving is permitted when supported by roleplay but must not be used to intentionally ruin another player's experience.

**3.2 — Vehicle Exploiting**
- Using vehicle glitches, unrealistic physics or exploits to gain an advantage is prohibited.

**3.3 — Vehicle Ramming**
- Intentionally ramming vehicles without appropriate roleplay justification is prohibited.
- Repeated unrealistic ramming during pursuits is not permitted.

**3.4 — Emergency Vehicles**
- Police, EMS and other emergency vehicles cannot be stolen or used without valid roleplay and server authorisation where required.

**3.5 — Vehicle Repairs**
- Players cannot use garages or repair mechanics to instantly escape an active roleplay situation.

**3.6 — Vehicle Storage**
- Storing, despawning or transferring vehicles to avoid active police or criminal roleplay is prohibited.

---

# 🔫 4. CRIMINAL ROLEPLAY RULES

**4.1 — Criminal Initiation**
- All criminal activity involving other players must have valid roleplay reasoning.
- Criminals must provide clear initiation before escalating to hostile actions.

**4.2 — Robbery Rules**
- Robberies must involve meaningful roleplay.
- Randomly robbing players repeatedly without reasonable character motivation is prohibited.
- Players cannot force others to withdraw money from banks or transfer assets unless specifically permitted by server policy.

**4.3 — Hostage Rules**
- Hostages must be genuine players involved in the situation.
- Fake hostages, arranged hostage situations and using friends as fake hostages are prohibited.
- Hostages must be treated fairly and given reasonable opportunities to roleplay.

**4.4 — Gang Roleplay**
- Gangs must follow all general server rules.
- Gang conflicts must have legitimate roleplay development.
- Random gang attacks and unnecessary escalation are prohibited.

**4.5 — Gang Wars**
- Gang wars must have a legitimate roleplay reason.
- Any formal war must follow the server's gang-war policy and staff requirements.
- Players cannot use gang wars as an excuse to ignore RDM, VDM or NLR rules.

**4.6 — Police Interactions**
- Criminals must allow reasonable opportunities for police roleplay.
- Players cannot deliberately provoke police repeatedly without meaningful roleplay.

**4.7 — Crime Locations**
- Criminal activity must respect designated safe zones and restricted areas.
- Players cannot exploit protected locations to escape consequences.

**4.8 — Robbing Emergency Services**
- Police and EMS equipment cannot be stolen unless explicitly allowed under server policy.
- EMS personnel cannot be targeted simply for performing medical duties.

**4.9 — Criminal Consequences**
- Criminal characters must accept reasonable consequences for their actions.
- Refusing to participate in legitimate arrest, investigation or prison roleplay is prohibited.

---

# 🚔 5. POLICE ROLEPLAY RULES

**5.1 — Professional Conduct**
- Police officers must maintain professional roleplay standards.
- Abuse of police powers is prohibited unless specifically approved as part of an organised storyline.

**5.2 — Reasonable Force**
- Officers must use force appropriate to the situation.
- Unnecessary escalation or excessive force is prohibited.

**5.3 — Traffic Stops**
- Traffic stops must have legitimate roleplay reasoning.
- Officers must provide reasonable opportunities for civilian interaction.

**5.4 — Arrest Procedures**
- Officers must follow department SOPs.
- Arrests, searches and seizures must have valid in-character justification under server law.

**5.5 — Police Corruption**
- Corrupt police roleplay requires management approval.
- Unauthorised corruption may result in removal from the department.

**5.6 — Police Equipment**
- Department equipment cannot be sold, traded or transferred to civilians without permission.

**5.7 — Police Pursuits**
- Officers must follow pursuit procedures and department policies.
- Unrealistic driving and unnecessary escalation are prohibited.

---

# 🚑 6. EMS & MEDICAL ROLEPLAY RULES

**6.1 — Respect EMS**
- EMS personnel must be allowed to perform legitimate medical roleplay.
- Targeting EMS without valid and approved roleplay justification is prohibited.

**6.2 — Medical Roleplay**
- Injuries must be roleplayed appropriately.
- Players cannot ignore serious injuries simply to continue fighting or escaping.

**6.3 — EMS Equipment**
- Medical equipment and emergency vehicles cannot be stolen or misused without permission.

**6.4 — Medical Revivals**
- Players must follow EMS instructions during treatment.
- Being revived does not automatically mean a character is fully recovered.

**6.5 — EMS Neutrality**
- EMS must provide fair treatment regardless of a patient's gang, criminal or police affiliation.

---

# 🏠 7. SAFE ZONE RULES

**7.1 — Designated Safe Zones**

Safe zones may include:
- Hospitals
- Police station public areas
- Spawn locations
- Dealerships
- Clothing stores
- Other staff-designated areas

**7.2 — No Hostile Activity**
- Robbing, attacking, kidnapping or initiating violent situations inside designated safe zones is prohibited.

**7.3 — Safe Zone Abuse**
- Players cannot run into safe zones to escape an active roleplay situation.
- Players cannot deliberately provoke others from protected areas.

**7.4 — Camping Safe Zones**
- Waiting outside safe zones solely to attack or rob players is prohibited.

---

# 💰 8. ECONOMY & BUSINESS RULES

**8.1 — Economy Exploits**
- Money duplication, item duplication and exploiting business systems are prohibited.

**8.2 — Business Ownership**
- Business owners must remain active and follow management requirements.
- Inactive businesses may be reviewed or reassigned by management.

**8.3 — Business Abuse**
- Businesses cannot be used to exploit the economy or generate money through unintended mechanics.

**8.4 — Scamming**
- In-character scams may only occur where explicitly permitted by server policy.
- Out-of-character scams and scams involving real money are prohibited.

**8.5 — Real Money Trading**
- Selling in-game money, items, vehicles or accounts for real-world money outside authorised server systems is prohibited.

---

# 💬 9. COMMUNICATION & DISCORD RULES

**9.1 — Respectful Communication**
- All players must communicate respectfully in-game and on Discord.
- Harassment, threats and discriminatory behaviour are prohibited.

**9.2 — Voice Chat**
- A working microphone is required for situations involving voice roleplay.
- Voice changers are allowed provided they do not disrupt roleplay.

**9.3 — No Mic Spam**
- Ear-rape, excessive screaming, soundboards and disruptive audio are prohibited.

**9.4 — Out-of-Character Communication**
- OOC discussions must not interfere with active roleplay.
- Discord calls cannot be used to exchange in-character information unfairly.

**9.5 — Advertising**
- Advertising other FiveM servers or Discord communities without permission is prohibited.

**9.6 — Staff Tickets**
- Support tickets must be used responsibly.
- False reports, ticket spam and harassment of staff are prohibited.

**9.7 — Privacy**
- Sharing another player's personal information without permission is strictly prohibited.

---

# ⚖️ 10. STAFF & ADMINISTRATION RULES

**10.1 — Staff Respect**
- Players must respect staff members and follow reasonable instructions.

**10.2 — Staff Abuse**
- Staff members cannot abuse permissions, commands or authority for personal gain.

**10.3 — Reporting Rule Breaks**
- Players should report rule violations through Discord tickets.
- Where possible, include video clips, screenshots or other relevant evidence.

**10.4 — No Taking Matters Into Your Own Hands**
- If another player breaks a rule, do not retaliate by breaking rules yourself.
- Continue roleplay where safe and report the incident afterwards.

**10.5 — Ban Appeals**
- Players may appeal eligible bans through the official Discord appeal system.
- Harassing staff over punishments is prohibited.

**10.6 — Final Decisions**
- Server management reserves the right to review punishments and update rules.
- All disciplinary actions should be fair and proportionate.

---

# 🚫 11. STRICTLY PROHIBITED BEHAVIOUR

The following behaviour may result in an immediate permanent ban:

- Cheating, mod menus or malicious exploits.
- Deliberately crashing or attacking server infrastructure.
- Doxxing or sharing private information.
- Serious harassment or discriminatory abuse.
- Threats of real-world violence.
- Real-money scams or fraudulent transactions.
- Repeated ban evasion.
- Deliberately exploiting the economy on a major scale.
